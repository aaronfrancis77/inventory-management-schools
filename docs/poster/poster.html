Daymap Inventory

Every laptop, lab kit and loan, tracked in one place.
A school inventory system built for Civica's Daymap platform.

01

THE PROBLEM

What are we addressing?

Schools manage thousands of shared assets with spreadsheets, paper sign-out sheets and memory.

  • Items go missingNo one knows who borrowed what.
  • No single viewStock is spread across rooms and campuses.
  • Stock expiresFirst-aid kits and chemicals go out of date.
  • One size doesn't fitA violin and an iPad need different details.
